Brief about the idea

Problem. In Sri Lanka, elders who speak only Tamil or Sinhala, and disabled neighbours, are skipped by gig and care apps: English-only, fast typing, cash in a stranger's hand, and no trusted way to check what a request really means.

Idea. Zivo lets people ask for help by speaking in their own language, and uses Gemini on Vertex AI as the trust-and-safety layer that makes strangers-helping-strangers safe.

The twist. The same AI lets blind, deaf and mobility-impaired neighbours be the helpers and earn — with tools that fit how they work.

One story

Kamala, 74, speaks Tamil → voice → plan (≈4 s) → safety → escrow (or Instant Dispatch on her block) → Nuwan (blind) + Access note → adapted brief + Zivo Eyes → chat on-platform (phones blocked) → Start/End OTP. Neighbours can Street Batch onto the same trip for less CO₂e.

Opportunities

How is it different?

  • Assistive AI tools (Be My Eyes, Seeing AI, Live Transcribe) help disabled people receive a service. Zivo puts them inside an earning marketplace.
  • Safety is judged in Tamil and Sinhala, not just English keywords.

How does it solve the problem?

  • Voice-first request → plan in one call.
  • Vernacular safety gate + escrow + OTPs: no cash handoff.
  • Ability-aware work: opt-in disclosure, adapted briefs, camera and doorstep tools.

USP

  • Ability never changes rank, price or pay — enforced in code and monitored for bias in BigQuery.
  • Gemini recommends; people decide; it can never move money.
  • Honest AI: "can't tell" instead of guessing expiry dates or medicine.

Context: WHO estimates ~1.3 billion people (~16 %) live with a significant disability. We do not claim sign-language translation — no Sri Lankan Sign Language model exists; we solve what Deaf helpers actually lack: understanding speech at the door.

Budget maths

  • Cloud Run scales to zero: no idle cost.

  • Sustainability: a 10 % fee on completed jobs funds the AI cost.

Additional details and future development

Responsible AI (in code)

  • Client assigns; Gemini only ranks and explains.
  • Money moves only by top-up, escrow hold, End-OTP release or cancel refund — no Gemini output is wired to it.
  • Ability never changes rank, price or pay; disclosure is opt-in; declines monitored (id-free events → BigQuery).
  • Safety fails to “review”, never “allow”.

Roadmap

  1. Shipped: Access audit · remote jobs · sighted-assist · chat phone block · Instant Dispatch · Street Batch
  2. Co-design with a Sri Lankan disability association; native-speaker review of ta/si copy
  3. Real ID verification; human appeal for safety blocks
  4. “Quick signals” experiment with the Deaf community (not translation)
