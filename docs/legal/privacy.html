Zivo Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026 · Soft launch / limited beta (Sri Lanka)

Zivo (“we”) provides on-demand neighbourhood help. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

Data we collect

  • Phone number and account profile (name, optional email)
  • Location when you post or accept nearby jobs
  • Task content, OTPs for job start/end, and wallet / payment records
  • KYC documents (NIC, selfie) for helper verification — reviewed by ops
  • Photos you upload as purchase proof (e.g. grocery receipts)

How we use data

  • Match clients and helpers, run escrow, prevent fraud
  • Process wallet top-ups via PayHere
  • Improve safety and support

Sharing

We share data with infrastructure providers (e.g. Supabase, SMS, PayHere) only as needed to operate the service. We do not sell personal data.

Retention & deletion

See Account deletion. You may request deletion of your account and associated personal data subject to legal retention (e.g. payment records).

Contact

Privacy questions: privacy@zivo.app
