Zivo Terms of Service

Last updated: August 2026 · Soft launch / limited beta (Sri Lanka)

The service

Zivo connects people who need short local help with nearby helpers. Escrow holds funds until job OTPs and (where required) purchase proof complete.

Accounts

You must provide accurate information and complete phone verification. Helper mode may require KYC approval.

Payments

Wallet top-ups are processed by PayHere. Platform fees apply on escrow release. Helpers must not take cash for goods covered by grocery/errand escrow.

Acceptable use

No illegal tasks, weapons, or prohibited content. We may suspend accounts that violate community guidelines.

Liability

During limited beta, the service is provided as-available. We are not liable for disputes between users beyond escrow and documented trust flows.
